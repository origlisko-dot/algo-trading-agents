I1. #1 Cross-sectional universe scanner job

Conf High

Cx M

Add os/jobs/universe_scan.py: score Finnhub-capped symbols with ML/momentum cross-section, emit top-N watchlist to runs/daily_watchlist.json, and make paper job consume that list instead of alphabetical Finnhub slice.

basis direct: universe loads 18k then caps 50 alphabetically (A, AA…); monitor/paper never exercise “ALL US” intent. Scan subjects named this gap.

why it matters: Without ranking, “full US universe” is theater — you trade liquid mega-caps by accident of sort order.

meeting_test: Yes — closes the stated product promise vs actual scan behavior.

I2. #2 FinMem closed loop: PnL → Chroma → next debate

Conf High

Cx M

On each portfolio snapshot, write structured reflections (weights, unrealized PnL, promotion winner) into Chroma; inject top-k recalls into agent graph / paper context next day.

basis external: FinMem layered memory + adjustable cognitive span; repo already has reflection + Chroma but not wired into next decision.

why it matters: Memory without retrieval is logging. Closing the loop is the FinMem differentiator the README claims.

meeting_test: Yes — turns Phase-4 self-improvement from aspiration into a daily job step.

I3. #3 Regime-conditioned promotion (not global Sharpe)

Conf Med

Cx M

Detect vol/trend regime on SPY; store per-regime Sharpe for momentum/agent/ml; promote the winner for the current regime into active_strategy.json.

basis reasoned: Global Sharpe on 5 symbols over 365d crowned ML once; that may be regime luck. ContestTrade + DataRobot C/C both assume continuous ranking under changing conditions.

why it matters: Prevents sticky wrong champion after a single quiet-tape bake-off.

meeting_test: Yes — upgrades the one promotion lever you already ship.

I4. #4 90-day paper ops dashboard

Conf High

Cx S

Static HTML (or Rich TUI) over pnl_history.jsonl + active strategy timeline + signal drift: equity, max DD, snapshots/day, strategy switches.

basis direct: pnl_tracker + portfolio job exist; Phase-2 roadmap needs 30–90 day evidence; no human-readable surface yet.

why it matters: Without a glanceable ops surface, paper discipline dies after week two.

meeting_test: Yes — cheapest path to “are we actually running Phase 2?”
